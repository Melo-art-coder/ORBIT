Privacy Policy

Please read this document carefully. This is ORBIT's working policy draft. It should be reviewed against the platform's final features and by a qualified Nigerian legal or data-protection professional before public launch.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ORBIT handles personal information when you visit or use the platform. We aim to collect only information needed to provide, maintain, and improve the Service.

This policy is a working description of the intended ORBIT product. Before launch, it must be checked against the actual live configuration, all connected services, and applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, ORBIT may handle:

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers, and information you provide when creating an account.
  • Profile and academic information: display name, full name, institution, faculty, department, programme, study level, academic session, semester, and selected theme.
  • Study information: course codes, course titles, credit units, and resources or notes you choose to add.
  • Saved items and activity: opportunity bookmarks, preferences, and information needed to remember your settings.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser, diagnostic, and usage information that may be generated when the Service operates.
  • Messages to us: information you include when contacting our support or privacy email.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information or another person’s information unless a feature requires it and you are authorized to provide it.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • create and secure accounts and authenticate users;
  • save profiles, courses, preferences, notes, and bookmarks;
  • provide the features users request;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal enquiries;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, and protect ORBIT;
  • prevent fraud, misuse, and security incidents; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not describe user information as being used for advertising personalization unless that practice is actually introduced and disclosed in an updated policy.

4. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate ORBIT, such as Supabase for authentication and database hosting. Those providers process information under their own service terms and applicable contractual arrangements.

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect users or the security of ORBIT, or in connection with a legitimate reorganization of the service, subject to applicable law. We do not make a user’s private academic profile public merely because they create an account.

5. Storage and security

ORBIT uses Supabase as part of its current authentication and database architecture. Depending on the configuration and services enabled, information may be stored or processed in data centers outside Nigeria. International processing and any required safeguards should be confirmed against the actual Supabase project settings before public launch.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No website, device, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should use a strong, unique password and keep their account credentials private.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain account records, resolve disputes, protect the platform, and meet legal obligations. The exact retention period can depend on the type of information and the feature involved.

When an account or item is deleted, information may remain temporarily in backups or records where necessary for security, recovery, or legal compliance. We will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify information when it is no longer required, subject to applicable law.

7. Your choices and rights

You can review and update profile information through available account settings. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to raise a concern about how it is handled. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may also have rights to object to or restrict certain processing, request portability, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant Nigerian data-protection authority.

8. Young users

ORBIT is designed as a student platform. If you are under the age at which you can legally provide consent for a particular type of data processing, you should involve a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law. We do not knowingly seek unnecessary personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

9. External links and services

ORBIT may contain links to universities, scholarship providers, employers, or other external services. When you leave ORBIT, the external service’s privacy policy applies. Review its terms before submitting information or applying.

10. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as ORBIT changes or legal requirements develop. We will update the “Last updated” date and take reasonable steps to highlight material changes. Please review this page periodically.
